# Preliminary Presence × Muse evidence

This reviewed evidence is included in the public repository. The analysis was frozen on 3 November 2026. The ratings come from one person in an unblinded pilot; the resource cohort also includes unrated records with uncertain origin and work comparability. The records span several tasks, models and software versions; repeated comparisons do not create independent participants.

Presence was rated easier in 27 of 31 comparisons; reported interruptions were lower in 23 of 31, equal in 4 and higher in 4. The mean effort score changed by −2.06 points on the 1–5 scale. These are self-reports, not a causal estimate or calibrated workload measurement.

Across 46 paired resource records, input was effectively unchanged (−0.60%) and output was 16% lower, while uncached input increased substantially. Cached input is already included in input; lower cache use is not itself a saving. Actual billing and full Presence/observer overhead remain unknown. Fifteen resource records have no paired ratings and uncertain origin/work comparability. Input histories can differ.

The inventory retains 77 saved comparison identities, including cancelled, incomplete, one-arm and no-paired-observed-work records. Duplicate exports count once per comparison. Only 31 cases supply paired ratings, 46 supply eligible paired resources and 34 complete paired timing. Individual redacted snapshots can cover 41 of 46 resource cases under the current format; five legacy cases lack required task/fixture metadata. The aggregate report keeps all eligible cases and does not select only compatible or favorable snapshots.

Latest code-check labels are stale or missing; earlier failures and later passes remain linked in the private history. Eight rated and three unrated resource comparisons retain earlier frozen-test failures. These results do not establish general technical equivalence, model compliance, a population effect, faster work or cost savings. Recorded guidance delivery and presentation mechanisms support narrower engineering claims only.

Original run identifiers, individual times, messages, source code, raw typing, Capsules and observer narrative are withheld. Public pseudonyms and policy controls can still be identifiable. Source commitments identify withheld bytes; they do not independently verify the original files. Historical runs do not qualify every feature of the final candidate or replace a fresh judge setup/login/coding/export rehearsal.
